## Design Direction

### Visual Style
- **Theme:** Dark mode with electric accent color (deep navy/charcoal background, bright cyan or electric blue accents)
- **Tone:** Modern, trustworthy, developer-friendly but also accessible to non-technical users
- **Feel:** Clean, fast, confident — like a product that actually works

### Typography
- Display font: Something bold and modern (e.g., Syne, Cabinet Grotesk, Clash Display)
- Body font: Something readable and clean (e.g., DM Sans, Plus Jakarta Sans)
- NOT: Inter, Roboto, Arial

### Colors
- Background: `#0A0F1E` (deep dark navy)
- Surface: `#111827`
- Accent: `#00D4FF` (electric cyan) or `#3B82F6` (electric blue)
- Text: `#F1F5F9`
- Muted text: `#94A3B8`
- Success: `#10B981`

### Animations
- Hero section: staggered fade-in on load
- Feature cards: hover lift effect
- How It Works steps: scroll-triggered reveal
- Chat widget mockup: animated typing indicator
- Pricing cards: hover scale on Pro card
