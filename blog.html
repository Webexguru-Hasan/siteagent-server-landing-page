Step-by-Step Guide: From Zero to AI in 5 Minutes

Setting up your website chatbot is a straightforward process designed for busy entrepreneurs.

Step 1: Sign Up and Start the Auto-Crawl

First, head over to SiteAgent and create your account. Once you're in, simply enter your website URL. SiteAgent’s AI will immediately begin to "crawl" your pages. It reads your FAQ, product descriptions, and service pages to understand exactly what your business does.

Step 2: Customize Your Widget Design

Brand consistency is key. Within the dashboard, you can customize the chatbot widget to match your site’s aesthetic. Change the primary colors, update the greeting (e.g., "Hi! I'm your AI shop assistant"), and choose whether you want the widget to appear on the bottom-right or bottom-left of the screen.

Step 3: Copy Your One-Line Snippet

Once you are happy with the preview, SiteAgent will generate a unique, one-line JavaScript code snippet. This single line of code is the "brain" that connects your website to your new AI assistant.

Platform Specific Instructions

Integrating SiteAgent into your favorite platform is remarkably simple. Here is how to handle the two most popular builders:

1. WordPress AI Chatbot Integration

For a WordPress AI chatbot, the easiest method is using a "Headers and Footers" plugin. Simply navigate to your plugin settings and paste your SiteAgent snippet into the Footer section. Alternatively, if you're comfortable with code, you can paste it directly into your theme's footer.php file just before the closing </body> tag.

2. Shopify AI Chatbot Integration

To set up your Shopify AI chatbot, go to Online Store > Themes > Edit Code. Find your theme.liquid file and paste the snippet just before the closing </head> or </body> tag. Save your changes, and your AI assistant will be live on every page of your store instantly.
